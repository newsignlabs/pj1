Our story

Three generations of bridal craftsmanship

What began as a small family workshop is today a trusted name for brides and their families. Every piece is designed in-house and finished by hand by our karigars, using techniques passed down through generations.

We take the time to understand your outfit, your ceremonies and your budget, so the jewellery you wear feels unmistakably yours.
